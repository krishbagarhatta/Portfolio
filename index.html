About

I like taking a messy problem — a pile of spreadsheets, a slow process, an unclear dataset — and turning it into something structured that people can actually use.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology at Lovely Professional University, School of Computer Science and Engineering. Alongside coursework, I build small, complete projects — a library system, a sales dashboard, a data analysis notebook — because I learn fastest by shipping something end-to-end rather than stopping at theory.

My core toolkit spans Python, C++ and Java for logic and data structures, SQL, MongoDB and MS SQL Server for storage, and Power BI, Tableau and ETL / Informatica IDQ for turning raw numbers into decisions. I'm equally comfortable writing an algorithm or explaining what a chart means to someone who isn't technical.
